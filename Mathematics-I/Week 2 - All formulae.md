# Week 2
## Formulae
### 1. Distance
$$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

### 2. Mid point 
$$x = \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \qquad y = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}$$

### 3. Slope 
$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$

### 4. Two point form
$$y - y_1 = \left(\frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\right)(x - x_1)$$

### 5. Slope point form
$$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$$

### 6. Slope intercept $y$
$$y = mx + c$$
### 7. Slope intercept $x$
$$y = m(x - d)$$

### 8. Intercept form
$$\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1$$

### 9. General equation form
$$ax + by + c = 0$$

### 10. Two lines are parallel
#### when:
$$m_1 = m_2$$
$$a_1\cdot b_2 = a_2\cdot b_1$$

### 11. Two lines are perpendicular
#### when:
$$m1\cdot m2 = -1$$
$$a_1\cdot a_2 = b_1 \cdot b_2$$

### 12. Distance b/w  a line and a point 
$$d = \frac{|ax_1 + by_1 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$$

### 13. Distance b/w two parallel lines 
$$d = \frac{|c_1 - c_2|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$$

### 14. Angle of $2$ intersection lines 
$$\tan\phi = \frac{|m_2 - m_1|}{1 + m_1m_2}$$

### 15. Area of Triangle $\triangle$
$$\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2}|x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)|$$

### 16. Sum Squared Error (SSE)
$$\sum_{i=1}^m\left(y - \hat{y}\right)^2$$
